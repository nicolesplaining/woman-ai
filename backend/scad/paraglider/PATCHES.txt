Local changes to the upstream Paraglider (flexible_flyer) files, see UPSTREAM_COMMIT.txt:

paraglider_palm_left.scad
  - Fixed a doubled comma in pin_coordinates that newer OpenSCAD parsers reject.
  - Added width_ratio: the palm is scaled by overall_scale*width_ratio across the hand (x)
    and overall_scale along its length (y) and thickness (z).

fingerator.scad
  - Added width_ratio with the same meaning; all finger scaling and tab clearance use it.

All pin and screw holes run along x, so their cross-sections are scaled only by the
length scale and stay round at the intended diameter.
