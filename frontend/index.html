Fitted

Sizing and gauntlet fitting for e-NABLE volunteers

Marker sheet: Letter A4

Size an e-NABLE hand from three photos of the arm.

Fitted measures the arm on the printed marker sheet, scales the Paraglider hand to the recipient's width and length, and builds a gauntlet around their forearm. You check the result and sign off before anything prints.

What you get

Hand
Paraglider palm, fingers and thumb, scaled separately in width and length. Joints stay standard size.
Gauntlet
Built from the measured forearm with a padding gap, a slip-on opening and strap slots.
Fit-test ring
A 15 mm slice of the gauntlet. Print it first: about 15 minutes instead of hours.
Build guide
Parts, print settings, assembly steps and a plain-language summary for the family.
Sign-off
Print files unlock after a volunteer checks the fit and signs with their name.
